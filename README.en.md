# Femboy Pill (凉澜丸)

![Large honey pill](./pics/damiwan.JPG)

## Project Overview

Femboy Pill is a formula made entirely from herbal ingredients and prepared as large honey pills. Its main ingredients include schisandra fruit, lily bulb, raspberry fruit, and cornel fruit (see the complete formula in [FORMULA.md](./FORMULA.md)). Femboy Pill is intended to enhance a particular kind of experience for a particular group: drawing in the floating heat, holding the edge, wrapping it in moisture, and building toward a fullness poised to unfold. The formula deliberately excludes warming and overly cloying ingredients and is intended to be gentler for people prone to internal heat or with weak digestion. The reference constitution for experimentation is yin deficiency with exuberant heat.

## Project Nature

Femboy Pill is not a reliable or mature formula. It is an experimental formula for private use and must not be used to treat disease or taken blindly. It is provided only for private reference and testing. Experiments involve risks and should be approached with caution.

## Current Status

Formula iteration: Version 2. Used in personal experiments (Version 2): more than 80 pills (9 g per pill). Personal rating: 6/10.

## Safety Warning

Some people may have allergic reactions to the herbs in Femboy Pill. For declining-function or yang-deficient constitutions (poor firmness, sensitivity to cold, and lethargy), Femboy Pill may worsen the underlying condition. Combining Femboy Pill with other medicines may cause side effects. Long-term or high-dose use may also cause side effects. Known possible side effects include joint soreness and urticaria (hives).
